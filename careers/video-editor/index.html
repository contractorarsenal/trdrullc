About TraderUInsider

TraderUInsider is a trading education community focused on helping traders improve their skills, understand market narratives, and become more disciplined operators.

We produce educational content, community resources, workshops, and market breakdowns designed to help our members improve their performance.

We're looking for someone who wants to help build a brand, not just edit videos.

The Role

As a Video Editor, you'll work directly with the TraderUInsider team to create high-quality content across multiple platforms.

You'll be responsible for turning raw footage into engaging content that keeps viewers watching, improves retention, and strengthens the TraderUInsider brand.

You should understand pacing, storytelling, motion graphics, social media trends, and how to make content feel engaging.

Responsibilities

  • Edit short-form content for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and X
  • Edit long-form educational content
  • Create engaging cuts that improve viewer retention
  • Add captions, graphics, transitions, and visual enhancements
  • Organize project files and maintain efficient workflows
  • Work directly with the team for revisions and content planning
  • Meet deadlines consistently
  • Help improve overall content quality and production value

Ideal Candidate

  • Strong video editing skills
  • Experience with Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or Final Cut Pro
  • Understanding of social media content
  • Strong attention to detail
  • Creative problem-solving ability
  • Reliable communication
  • Ability to work in person
  • Located in or willing to relocate to the Greater Seattle Area

Requirements

Portfolio Required

Applicants must submit examples of previous work.

In-Person Requirement
