FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Trader U?

Trader U is a memecoin trading community and education platform. You learn how to find opportunities, read the market, plan entries and exits, manage risk, and build your own process, alongside a community of active traders.

Is this beginner friendly?

Yes. Trader U starts with wallet setup and market basics before moving into narratives, entries, exits, and risk management.

Is this a signals group?

No. Trader U is not a blind signals or copy-trading group. You learn how to research opportunities, understand setups, manage risk, and make your own trading decisions.

Are workshops included?

No. Workshops are separate products and are not included in the Trader U membership.

Is this a monthly subscription?

No. Trader U is a one-time payment, plus applicable taxes at checkout, with lifetime access to the core education.

What happens after I purchase?

You get immediate access to the Trader U curriculum and community right after checkout.

What is the refund policy?

All purchases are final and non-refundable because members receive immediate access to the curriculum, tools, community, and digital resources. Trading results are never guaranteed.
